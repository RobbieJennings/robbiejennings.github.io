Welcome to my blog!

A space for me to post my photography, document my code and share my travels and thoughts. I run an old-school black & white darkroom in my garden shed and a new-school Kubernetes and Nixos based homelab in my office.

Previously, I have worked professionally as a backend engineer at Murex and Aerlytix with experience in building responsive APIs for modern web services as well as database management.

My camera collection includes a Nikon FE, Rolleiflex Automat and Bronica SQ-A. My darkroom consists of a Durst M605 Colour enlarger and Ilford pop-up darkroom tent. I also digitise my prints and negatives using an Epson v550 flatbed scanner and Vuescan software.

My homelab serves all kinds of useful applications including Nextcloud, Forgejo, Immich, Jellyfin and the suite of Servarr applications. All of this accessible anywhere in the world using my Netbird mesh network.

If any of this sounds interesting to you then you're in the right place! I hope you enjoy my projects and stories.
